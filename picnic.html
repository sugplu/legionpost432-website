A Cambria tradition

Fourth of July Picnic in the Park

Every Independence Day, Post 432 invites the whole town — neighbors, families and visitors — to Shamel Park to celebrate our country's birthday together.

Thank you, Cambria!

The 45th annual Picnic — on America's 250th birthday

A heartfelt thank you to our community and our generous sponsors for making the 2026 American Legion Family Picnic in the Park such a great success. Your support, generosity and participation made it a wonderful celebration for everyone.

We also thank our dedicated volunteers. From planning and setup to serving and cleanup, your hard work did not go unnoticed. We look forward to celebrating with you again next year!

Save the date: the 46th annual Picnic in the Park is Sunday, July 4, 2027 at Shamel Park. Schedule and volunteer sign-ups will be posted here in the spring.

What to expect

A day at the Picnic

Based on the 2026 schedule. The 2027 schedule will be posted closer to the day.

11:00 AM
Opening ceremonies at the flagpole
11:30 AM
Folklorico dancers
12–3 PM
Waiter & waitress relay races, Linn's Pie Eating Contest, and old-fashioned races for kids
Late afternoon
Raffle drawings
5:00 PM
Picnic ends — in time to catch fireworks elsewhere

All day: live DJ, face painting, games, and food and drinks from the Legion — every purchase supports Post 432's programs.

Getting there

Shamel Park

5455 Windsor Blvd., Cambria — where Windsor Blvd. meets the ocean.

Parking is limited, so please carpool or ride the Cambria trolley, which runs a Moonstone / Main Street route on the Fourth with no set stops — just wave it down.

Map to Shamel Park

Please, no fireworks and no personal barbecues in the park.

Let's all have a good time

Picnic do's and don'ts

Do

  • Bring sunscreen, lawn chairs and layers — it's the Central Coast!
  • Carpool or ride the trolley, and park legally
  • Join the games and the pie eating contest
  • Buy raffle tickets and food on site to support the Legion
  • Keep pets on a leash, and take your trash with you
  • Volunteer to help — and tell your friends and family

Don't

  • Bring your own barbecue
  • Bring fireworks
  • Leave your trash behind
  • Forget your wallet or phone charger
