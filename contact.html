Good to know

Frequently asked

Can I rent the Veterans Memorial Hall?

The Veterans Memorial Hall is owned and managed by the Cambria Community Services District (CCSD), so rentals are booked through them, not through the Legion. See the CCSD Veterans Memorial Hall page for rooms, rates and the reservation form.

Is Club 432 open to the public?

Club 432 is for Legion Family members and their guests. Our monthly pancake breakfast (third Saturday, 8–11 AM) and Auxiliary Bingo (second Wednesday) welcome everyone. They're a great way to meet us.

When and where are meetings?

General meetings are the first Wednesday of each month at 6:00 PM in the Legion Dining Room, with dinner at 5:00 PM. Meetings are open to the public.

How do I let you know a member has passed away?

We're sorry for your loss. Please call (805) 927-5010 or use the form above and choose “Reporting a member's passing.” We'll make sure your loved one is honored in our Post Everlasting.
