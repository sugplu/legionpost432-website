The American Legion

Who can join

You're eligible if you served at least one day of active military duty since December 7, 1941, and were honorably discharged or are still serving honorably. You don't need to have served overseas or in combat.

  • Army
  • Navy
  • Marine Corps
  • Air Force
  • Space Force
  • Coast Guard
  • National Guard and Reserves: you may be eligible if you served on federal active-duty orders (such as Title 10 orders). Not sure? Bring your paperwork and we'll help you check.
  • U.S. Merchant Marine: those who served between December 7, 1941, and December 31, 1946, may also qualify.

How to join

  1. Download the applicationPost 432 membership application (PDF) — or pick one up at the Club.
  2. Attach a copy of your DD-214Lost yours? Request a free copy from the National Archives.
  3. Mail it with your checkAmerican Legion Post 432, P.O. Box 697, Cambria, CA 93428-0697 — or bring it to a general meeting.

Already a Legionnaire somewhere else? Use the same form to transfer to Post 432. You can also join online at legion.org/join — then give us a call so we can welcome you to Post 432.

Sons of The American Legion

Honor your family's service

You can join the Sons of The American Legion if you are a male descendant — including sons, stepsons and adopted sons — of:

  • a member of The American Legion, or
  • a veteran who served during an eligible period and died while serving, or who later received an honorable discharge.

Make checks payable to “SAL Squadron 432” and mail with your application to P.O. Box 697, Cambria, CA 93428-0697. Learn more about Squadron 432.

$30 / year

American Legion Auxiliary

“Service, Not Self”

The American Legion Auxiliary welcomes family members of veterans who are eligible for The American Legion. Auxiliary members support veterans, military families, young people and our community — and Unit 432 puts on our Thursday night dinners and monthly Bingo.

Eligibility rules depend on your relationship to the veteran, so the easiest way to find out is to ask. Call the Post or send us a message and an Auxiliary officer will help. Learn more about Unit 432.

Questions

Common questions

Do I have to live in Cambria?

No. You can belong to any American Legion post you like. Many of our members live in Cambria, Cayucos, San Simeon and nearby towns.

I can't find my DD-214. What do I do?

Veterans and next of kin can request military records free of charge from the National Archives. The San Luis Obispo County Veterans Services Office can also help.

What do I get as a member?

Fellowship with fellow veterans, Club 432 privileges for you and your guests, The Bugle newsletter, The American Legion Magazine, member discounts from The American Legion — and the satisfaction of serving your community alongside people who understand.

Can I visit before I join?

Absolutely. Come to a pancake breakfast on the third Saturday of the month, or a general meeting on the first Wednesday at 6 PM. Introduce yourself — we'd love to meet you.
