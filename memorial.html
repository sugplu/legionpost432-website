Cambria Veterans Memorial

A place to remember, in the heart of town

Honoring the service and sacrifice of all our nation's past, present and future military members — with special emphasis on the veterans who call Cambria home.

The memorial

Dedicated Memorial Day, 2011

The Cambria Veterans Memorial was dedicated on Memorial Day, May 30, 2011. The Flag Memorial stands in the parking lot of the Veterans Memorial Hall at 1000 Main Street, where its flags fly over our town day and night.

The memorial was designed to recognize everyone who has served, and to reflect the character and charm of our community. Each Memorial Day, Post 432 gathers here to honor those who gave their lives in service and to remember our Post Everlasting.

Engraved bricks

Secure your place in Cambria's history

For a donation of $125 per brick, you can honor a veteran, a family or a loved one with an engraved brick on the Post's flag monument. You don't have to be a veteran to order one — any individual, business or organization is welcome.

Preview your brick

Up to 3 lines and 16 characters per line, including spaces. Letters are engraved in capitals; leave out periods and use “&” for “and.” Text is centered automatically.
