Who we are

A close-knit family, serving a close-knit town

Here in Cambria, the word “family” means something special. Post 432 is a group of men and women who come together to honor and serve our veterans, support our community and look out for one another. Because Cambria is a small and somewhat isolated town, we believe in taking care of each other — we work together, share meals, celebrate together and lend a hand whenever it's needed.

When you join us, you may find you receive far more than you give: wonderful people, inspiring speakers, a chance to cook, bake or barbecue, to support local kids, and to make a real difference right here at home.
